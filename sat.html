TEACHING & ASSESSMENT

Teaching tells me where practice material needs to work harder.

When many students miss the same question for different reasons, an answer key is not enough.

The practice library and question-development work are built around the same principle used in teaching: identify the reasoning, locate the failure point, and make the next practice decision more useful.

PRACTICE PHILOSOPHY

Difficulty should come from reasoning, not unnecessary complication.

Practice should make the real test feel easier, not merely familiar.

A useful hard question connects familiar SAT ideas in a way the student has not seen packaged before. The goal is stronger recognition and decision-making, not ugly arithmetic.
