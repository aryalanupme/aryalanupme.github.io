HOW THIS PORTFOLIO IS ORGANIZED

Categories first. Case studies where the details matter.

Not every project needs a full page, and repeating similar installations would make the portfolio harder to read.

Each engineering category can contain a few detailed case studies supported by photographs, drawings, survey data or reports, while the remaining projects can appear as concise project cards. This keeps the site clean even as the portfolio grows.
